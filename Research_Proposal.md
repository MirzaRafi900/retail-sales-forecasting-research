# Research Proposal

## Title

A Comparative Study of Machine Learning Models for Retail Sales Forecasting

## Background

Retail businesses rely on accurate Sales forecasting for inventory management, financial planning, and business decision-making.

Machine learning techniques provide opportunities for improving forecasting accuracy through the analysis of historical sales date.

## Problem Statement

Organizations face challenges in accurately forecasting future sales due to changing customer behaviour and market trends.

## Research Objectives

1. Analyze retail sales patterns.
2. Identify key factors influencing sales.
3. Develop forecasting models.
4. Compare forecasting performance.

## Research Questions

RQ1: Which factors influence retail sales performance?

RQ2: How do category and region affect sales trends?

RQ3: Can machine learning models accurately forecast future sales?

RQ4: Which model performs best?

Superstore Retail Dataset
