# Literature Review Comparison

## Paper 1

Title:

Sales Forecasting using Machine Learning Algorithms in the Retailer Sector

Key Findings:

XBoost achieved the best result in forecasting performance compared to ARIMA and FB Prophet.

## Paper 2

Title: 

Comparative Analysis of Modern Machine Learning Models for Retail Sales Forecasting

Key Findings:

In this research, XBoost had outperformed deep learning models and statistical forecasting approaches.

## Common Findings

1. Both of the studies mainly focused on Retail Sales Forecasting.
2. Both of the studies compared multiple forecasting methods.
3. Both of the studies identified XBoost as one of the strongest and most reliable models for forecasting.
4. Machine Learning methods generally outperformed traditional forecasting techniques.
5. Data quality and feature engineering significantly influenced forecasting performance.
