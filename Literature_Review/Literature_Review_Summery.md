# Literature Review Comparison

## Overview

Retail sales forecasting is a significant area of research in data analytics and machine learning. Accurate forecasting supports inventory management, financial planning, customer demand estimation, and business decision-making.

## Comparison of Reviewed Studies

### Study 1

Title:
Sales Forecasting Using Machine Learning Algorithm in the Retail Sector

Models Evaluated:
• XGBoost
• ARIMA
• FB Prophet

Key Finding:
XGBoost achieved the highest forecasting performance among the evaluated methods.

---

### Study 2

Title:
Comparative Analysis of Modern Machine Learning Models for Retail Sales Forecasting

Models Evaluated:
• XGBoost
• LightGBM
• N-BEATS
• N-HiTS
• TFT

Key Finding:
XGBoost outperformed both deep learning and statistical forecasting approaches.

---

## Common Findings

1. Both studies focused on retail sales forecasting.

2. Both studies compared multiple forecasting approaches.

3. XGBoost consistently demonstrated strong forecasting performance.

4. Data preprocessing and feature engineering significantly affect model performance.

5. Machine learning approaches generally outperformed traditional forecasting methods.

## Research Observation

The repeated success of XGBoost suggests that tree-based machine learning approaches are highly effective for retail sales forecasting tasks.

## Preliminary Research Gap

Most existing studies focus on advanced models such as:

• XGBoost
• LightGBM
• FB Prophet
• ARIMA
• Deep Learning Models

However, fewer studies compare simpler machine learning approaches such as:

• Linear Regression
• Decision Tree Regression
• Random Forest Regression

within a unified retail forecasting framework.

This may provide an opportunity for further investigation.
