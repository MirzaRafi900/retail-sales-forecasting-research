## Preliminary Research Gap

Most studies compare XGBoost against time-series and deep learning models.

However, fewer studies examine and compare simple machine learning methods such as:

- Linear Regression
- Decision Tree Regression
- Random Forest Regression

Using the same retail sales forecasting dataset.

This research aims to investigate whether simpler machine learning approaches can provide competitive forecasting performance.

### models:

But fewer studies investigate and compare simple learning approaches like:
- Linear Regression
- Decision Tree Regression
- Random Forest Regression

In the same forecasting dataset.

In this research, they tried to prove that the simpler models can also perform and provide competitive forecasting.
